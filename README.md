# Health → Family → Wealth

> Overall I believe the formula to success is 1. health 2. family 3. wealth.
> But I am doing things the other way around because cashflow always seems to be an issue.

This repo is the reminder: real success is good health and a good relationship with my family. Wealth follows.

## Where I'm starting from (Thu 22 Oct 2026)

Written at the golf course with a burrito, a beer, and my smokes in my pocket.

- Smoking again
- Drinking too much
- Little exercise
- No skincare routine
- Need to lose weight
- My wife is losing patience with me, or already has

This is a process, not a quick fix. But the changes need to be big.

## Day 1: Monday 26 October 2026

## The rules

1. **Not a single puff.**
2. **No beer.** Weeks 1–2: no alcohol at all. From week 3: one glass of wine, with her, at the table.
3. **Gym every day, 16:00–17:00.** Weights Mon / Wed / Fri, cardio and core Tue / Thu / Sat, easy recovery on Sunday. Walk after dinner.
4. **Skin twice a day.** Morning: cleanse, moisturise, SPF. Night: cleanse, moisturise. Two minutes.
5. **Show, don't promise.** She'll believe day 30, not day 1.

## Why this order works

Smoking, beer and the golf course are one habit, not three. The beer brings out the cigarettes, and both pull the food along with them. Break the beer and the smokes get easier.

Health first doesn't cost wealth. It pays straight away: every week without cigarettes and beer is money I didn't burn.
