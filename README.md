# Health → Family → Wealth

> Overall I believe the formula to success is 1. health 2. family 3. wealth.
> But I am doing things the other way around because cashflow always seems to be an issue.

This repo is the reminder: real success is good health and a good relationship with my family. Wealth follows.

## Where I'm starting from (Thu 22 Oct 2026)

Written at the golf course with a burrito, a beer, and my smokes in my pocket.

- Smoking again
- Drinking too much
- Little exercise
- No skincare routine
- Need to lose weight
- My wife is losing patience with me, or already has

This is a process, not a quick fix. But the changes need to be big.

The patch goes on before that, on Friday 23 October, while I'm still smoking.

## The rules

1. **Not a single puff.** If I slip, the patch stays on, the rest goes in the bin, and I tell her the same day.
2. **No beer, ever. No alcohol at the club.** No alcohol at all until Day 28. From Mon 23 Nov: one agreed glass of wine with her, at home, and never on golf days.
3. **Gym every day, 16:00–17:00.** Weights Mon / Wed / Fri, cardio and core Tue / Thu / Sat, easy recovery on Sunday. A brisk walk after dinner.
4. **Skin twice a day.** Morning: cleanse, moisturise, SPF. Night: cleanse, moisturise. Two minutes.
5. **Hold my weight until Day 90.** No dieting while I quit. Fat loss starts in February.
6. **She watches, I report.** She notices; she doesn't check, quiz or remind. Keeping me accountable is my job.

## Why this order works

Smoking, beer and the golf course are one habit, not three. On drinking days the risk of a smoking slip is about five times higher, and those slips happen in bars, with friends, around other smokers. Break the beer and the smokes get easier.

Health first doesn't cost wealth. It pays straight away: every week without cigarettes and beer is money I didn't burn.
