# Skincare and skin checks in Cape Town's Southern Suburbs: clinics, costs, starter routine (research as of 22 Oct 2026)

Context for the report writer: these notes are for a man in his late 30s or 40s in Cape Town. He has years of sun exposure from golf and has never had a skincare routine. He is quitting smoking and drinking from Mon 26 Oct 2026. He wants three things: a skin consultation, with a calendar hold for Wed 11 Nov 2026; a full skin and mole check, which is the medical priority; and a simple daily routine. Cash is tight.

Rules followed:
- Every clinic and price below comes from a page that was opened on 22 Oct 2026. The URL is given.
- Retail prices are "as listed 22 Oct 2026". Clinic fees are "as listed" on the page, with the page's own date where one is shown.
- Where nothing was found, the notes say so.
- No phone number is given unless it appears on the cited page.

These notes are not medical advice. Any spot that is new, changing, bleeding or not healing should be seen by a doctor, whatever the budget.

---

## 0. Quick answer

- **Medical priority (mole and skin-cancer check).** A doctor using a dermatoscope is the right tool.
  - **Cheapest doctor option found:** Tokai Skin. Dr Tammi Valentine is a GP with a postgraduate diploma in dermatology and a dermoscopy/skin-cancer certificate. A skin-cancer exam is **R850**.
  - **Specialist dermatologists with published fees:** Dermatology House, Newlands, **R1,650** first consult. Dr Orrey Derma Clinic, Constantiaberg/Steenberg, **R1,905**.
  - **Cheapest screen of any kind:** CANSA's nurse-led mole analysis, **R100**, or **R200** with FotoFinder. It is in Athlone, not the Southern Suburbs, and is not a doctor's exam.
- **Routine advice.** Several Southern Suburbs skin clinics offer a **free** consult: The Skin Bar (Kenilworth), Novaderm (Claremont) and Glow Aesthetics (Rondebosch). Artisan Skin in Plumstead offers a Visia skin analysis "from R0". Dermalogica at Cavendish Square offers a free 5-minute "face mapping".
- **Starter routine.** A cleanser, moisturiser and oil-control SPF50 from Clicks or Dis-Chem costs about **R390–R440**, or about **R340–R390** with current specials.
- **Pharmacy clinics.** Neither Clicks nor Dis-Chem clinic menus list a skin or mole check. Clicks clinics do run a **GoSmokeFree** quit-smoking programme, which is relevant from 26 Oct.

**GP referral and medical aid**
- Private dermatologists in South Africa can be booked directly. Dermatology House, Cape Town Dermatologists and Dermaclinic all take self-referrals through their forms. Dermaclinic's form asks whether you were referred, but that is optional.
- **Discovery Health 2025 plan comparison:** on the network or entry plans (KeyCare and similar), specialist cover is "up to R5,550 per person per year when referred by your nominated GP". On Executive, Comprehensive, Priority and Saver, day-to-day costs such as GP visits are paid from the medical savings account "as long as you have money available". — [Discovery 2025 plan comparison PDF](https://www.discovery.co.za/wcm/discoverycoza/assets/faz/medical-aid/2025/benefit-information/discovery-health-medical-scheme-plan-comparison.pdf)
- A third-party 2026 guide says hospital-only plans cover skin-cancer surgery but not outpatient consults. It also says state hospital dermatology (Groote Schuur) is free after a clinic or GP referral, with routine waits of 4–8 months and suspected cancers fast-tracked. — [ScanSkinAI Cape Town guide](https://www.scanskinai.com/za/skin-check-cape-town) (secondary marketing site; treat as indicative)
- **Practical point:** Dermatology House and Tokai Skin both require payment on the day and leave you to claim it back. If he has medical aid, he should check his plan's referral rule and specialist rate before booking.

- **Yes, CANSA does skin screening:**
  - "Clinical Mole Analysis" is a trained professional nurse checking moles against the ABCDE guide. **R100 per person.**
  - "FotoFinder Mole Analysis" adds an electronic device that "measures the risk profile of the mole", and a record can be kept for future comparison. **R200 per person.**
  - Prices are from the March 2026 price list and include VAT.
- CANSA cannot do biopsies. It gives "a referral letter… to a suitably qualified medical practitioner".
- The FotoFinder equipment "roams between CANSA Care Centres", so book ahead and ask whether it will be at your centre on your date.
- CANSA accepts most medical aids, with pre-authorisation required.
- **Nearest centre:** CANSA Cape Metro Care Centre, 7 Dunbar Avenue, **Athlone**, just outside the Southern Suburbs. Tel 021 689 5347. Bookings: capemetro@cansa.org.za. Office hours Mon–Fri 08:00–16:30.
- **Dates:** I found no published upcoming Cape Town skin-screening date for Nov 2026. Screening at the Care Centre is by appointment.
- The big free public screening days were in **September 2026**:
  - Skin Cancer Foundation of SA National Skin Cancer Screening Day, 1 Sept 2026 (search snippets only).
  - BIODERMA's "third annual Skin Health Circuit", free screenings at **Canal Walk, Cape Town, on 5 Sept 2026**. — [IOL, 26 Aug 2026](https://iol.co.za/lifestyle/health/2026-08-26-catch-the-rays-not-the-risk-free-skin-cancer-screenings-this-september/)
