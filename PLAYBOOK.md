# Playbook

## Smoking

**The rule: not a single puff.**

Get help; it isn't weakness. Stop-smoking medicine plus some support can make you up to three times more likely to quit than willpower alone.

- **Combination NRT:** a patch for a steady base, plus gum, lozenge or spray for cravings.
- **Or prescription medicine:** varenicline or cytisine. Ask the GP.
- **Support:** your national quitline, your pharmacist, or a local stop-smoking service. Most are free.

**Patches won't keep you hooked if you step down on schedule.** The smoke is what does the damage, not the nicotine. A patch releases nicotine slowly, without the hit a cigarette gives you in seconds, so it's hard to get addicted to. The patch handles the chemical side while you break the habits (beer, golf, after meals). Then you taper off:

- Highest strength for about 6–8 weeks.
- Step down over the next 2–4 weeks.
- Off the patches by Day 90 (Sat 23 Jan 2027).

Use the gum or lozenge only for real cravings and taper it alongside the patches. Don't switch to vapes or nicotine pouches. They deliver the fast hit and keep you hooked.

**When a craving hits** (it passes in a few minutes):

1. **Delay.** Tell yourself five minutes.
2. **Breathe.** Ten slow, deep breaths.
3. **Drink** a glass of water.
4. **Do something:** walk, do push-ups, call someone, use the gum or lozenge.

**Triggers and swaps**

| Trigger | Swap |
|---|---|
| Beer | No beer. That's why it's off. |
| Golf: at the turn, after a bad hole | Gum or lozenge in the bag, water bottle, walk the course |
| Coffee | Change the routine for a couple of weeks: different place, or tea |
| After meals | Get up from the table: brush your teeth or go for a walk |
| Stress and cashflow worries | Ten breaths, then a walk around the block |
| Driving | Gum in the car; no cigarettes in the car, ever |

**If you slip:** one cigarette is a slip, not a relapse. Don't buy a pack and don't "start again next Monday". Go back to the rule at the next craving, work out what triggered the slip, and plan for it next time.

**Weight:** most people put on a few kilos after quitting. It's expected, and dropping beer plus training covers it. It's not a reason to smoke.

## Alcohol

- **Weeks 1–2:** none. This protects the smoking quit.
- **From week 3:** beer stays off. One glass of wine with her, at the table, on agreed evenings. Not at golf, not alone, not "just one more".
- **Golf, camp, social:** soda water and lime, or alcohol-free beer if it doesn't make you want a smoke.
- ⚠️ If you've been drinking heavily every day and get shakes, sweats, a racing heart or bad anxiety after stopping, see a doctor. Stopping suddenly after heavy daily drinking can be dangerous.

## Training

The goal is to get stronger, lose fat, and feel better. Your golf will improve too.

- **Mon / Wed / Fri:** weights, alternating workouts A and B. About 45 minutes.
- **Every day:** a 30-minute walk. On golf days, walk the course.
- **Warm-up:** 5 minutes on a bike or rower, then a couple of light sets of the first exercise.
- **Weeks 1–2:** 2 sets of each exercise. **Week 3 onwards:** 3 sets.
- **Choosing a weight:** end each set feeling you could do 2–3 more reps. Once you hit the top of the rep range on every set, go up a little next time.
- A few sessions with a trainer to learn form is money well spent.

**Workout A**

| Exercise | Sets × reps |
|---|---|
| Goblet squat | 3 × 8–10 |
| Dumbbell bench press (or push-ups) | 3 × 8–10 |
| Seated cable row | 3 × 10–12 |
| Plank | 3 × 30 s |
| Farmer's carry | 3 × 30 m |

**Workout B**

| Exercise | Sets × reps |
|---|---|
| Romanian deadlift (dumbbells) | 3 × 8–10 |
| Lat pulldown | 3 × 10–12 |
| Dumbbell shoulder press | 3 × 8–10 |
| Reverse lunge | 2 × 8 each leg |
| Side plank | 2 × 20 s each side |

**Rotation:** week 1 is A / B / A, week 2 is B / A / B, and so on.

## Food

This isn't a diet. It's a few rules, added in steps.

**Weeks 1–2 (while quitting):**

- Protein at every meal: a palm-sized portion of eggs, meat, fish, yoghurt or beans.
- A big glass of water before each meal.
- No beer. That's already the biggest cut.
- For snack cravings: fruit, a small handful of nuts, sugar-free gum.

**Week 3 onwards:**

- Half the plate vegetables at dinner.
- The kitchen closes after dinner.
- **Golf days:** eat before you go, and choose the lighter option at the course. The burrito isn't the problem; burrito, beer and smokes as the default is.
- Weigh in on Monday mornings under the same conditions and write it in [LOG.md](LOG.md). Watch the trend, not single days.

## Skin

Golf means years of sun. Smoking and drinking show on your skin too, so quitting both will make a visible difference.

- **Morning (1 min):** cleanser (or just water), moisturiser, SPF 30–50.
- **Night (1 min):** cleanser, moisturiser.
- **On the course:** an SPF stick in the golf bag, reapplied at the turn, plus a hat.

Keep the products next to your toothbrush so the routine rides on a habit you already have.

**Clinic visit (book now, go in weeks 2–3):**

- A full skin and mole check, given all those years on the course.
- A skin assessment and a routine built for you. They'll probably add a retinoid at night and maybe a vitamin C serum in the morning.
- Pay for it with the money you're no longer spending on cigarettes and beer.

## Family

She'll believe day 30, not day 1.

- **Show, don't promise.** No big speeches. Do the thing, every day.
- **Warn her about week 1.** Nicotine withdrawal makes you irritable. Tell her it's coming and that it isn't about her.
- **Be present.** Phone away at dinner.
- **One thing together each week:** a walk, a dinner out, the glass of wine (from week 3).
- **Don't ask for credit.** If she notices, great. If she doesn't yet, keep going.

## Money

Putting health first doesn't cost wealth; it starts paying straight away.

- Work out your weekly spend on cigarettes and beer, plus food and drinks at the course. Write it in [LOG.md](LOG.md).
- Every week, move that amount into a separate account or savings pot.
- Spend the first of it on the skincare clinic. After that, let it build. That's cashflow you didn't have before.
